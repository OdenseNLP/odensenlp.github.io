title: Continual Quantization-Aware Pre-Training accepted to ACL 2025 Findings
---

**Continual Quantization-Aware Pre-Training: When to transition from 16-bit to 1.58-bit pre-training for BitNet language models?** by Jacob Nielsen, Peter Schneider-Kamp, and Lukas Galke has been accepted to the Findings of ACL 2025.

[Read the paper in the ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.694/).
