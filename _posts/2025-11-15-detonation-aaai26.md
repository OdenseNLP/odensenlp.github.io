**DeToNATION: Decoupled Torch Network-Aware Training on Interlinked Online Nodes** by Mogens Henrik From, Jacob Nielsen, Lukas Galke, and Peter Schneider-Kamp has been accepted to AAAI 2026.

[Read the paper in the AAAI proceedings](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/39256).
