---
title: DFM Mimir v1 released
---

[Danish Foundation Models](https://www.foundationmodels.dk/) has released **Mimir v1**, a **1-billion-parameter language model** based on the Hierarchical Reasoning Model (HRM) architecture and trained from scratch on permissible and openly licensed data.

Mimir is optimised for **Danish and English** and was trained on a mixture of **161 datasets**. Across 20 benchmarks covering English, mathematics and code, and Danish, it outperforms the original HRM-Text 1B and achieves particularly strong results for Danish.

The work originated within **OdenseNLP** and was developed as part of the Danish Foundation Models project.

The model is available on [HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co/danish-foundation-models/DFM-Mimir).

Read the full [release note on the DFM Website](https://www.foundationmodels.dk/news/2026/08/14/mimir-1-release-note.html) and the technical report [here](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13517).
