OdenseNLP was at LREC 2026 and authored/contributed in three papers: 

1. [DaLA: Danish Linguistic Acceptability Evaluation Guided by Real World Errors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.04799). A method and dataset for evaluationg Danish linguistic acceptability capabilities of LLMs focusing on real-world common errors made by Danish speakers. 
2. [Dynaword: From One-shot to Continuously Developed Datasets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.02271). A framework for creating large-scale, open datasets that can be continuously updated through community collaboration and the Danish Dynaword.
3. [SommBench: Assessing Sommelier Expertise of Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12117). A multilingual benchmark to evaluate LLMs sommelier expertise.
